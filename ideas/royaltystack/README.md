## Idea summary

**Target customer:** Digital-download sellers running the same templates/printables/patterns across 2+ of Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip, and Creative Fabrica.

**Core pain:** Each platform pays out on a different schedule with a different CSV export format. Tax time means manually reconciling payout records/1099s from every platform by hand.

**MVP approach:** CSV-upload only (no bank/API linking) — normalizes exports into one earnings dashboard + a tax-summary export. Zero out-of-pocket cost to build/run: no Plaid-style per-account fees, no paid market-data feeds; hosting/DB/email all fit on free tiers early on.

**Revenue model:** $9-19/mo or ~$79/yr, plus a $20 one-time quarterly tax-summary export add-on.

**Validator notes:**
- Market is real but niche (likely low tens of thousands of qualifying sellers globally, not millions).
- No competitor spans this specific platform combination — Stock Performer owns stock-photo-only, Craftybase owns Etsy-only.
- The real competitor is the spreadsheet habit, not a paid product.
- Ongoing cost is parser-maintenance labor (platform CSV formats drift), not dollars.
- Distribution path: r/EtsySellers, Creative Fabrica community, Etsy seller Facebook groups, KDP/self-publishing groups, SEO content on searches like "Etsy multi-shop bookkeeping."
