## BundleGuard — get your Amazon bundles compliant before Jan 11, 2027
- **The change (announced Sept 2026):** from **January 11, 2027**, most physical bundles under one ASIN must be **packaged by the original manufacturer or brand**. The exceptions:
  - written authorization (LOA) from each brand to repackage
  - gift and camera categories
  - Amazon Virtual Bundles, which need Brand Registry

  Non-compliant bundles get **deactivated regardless of remaining stock** and logged as Account Health violations ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/amazon-gives-bundle-sellers-4-months-to-get-brand-approval-or-lose-listings/), [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-product-bundling-rules-change-on-january-11-2027/), [Seller Essentials](https://selleressentials.com/amazon-bundle-policy-2027/), [DAM Law](https://damlawfirm.com/blog/amazon-bundling-policy-january-2027/)).
- **Gap:** only blogs and law-firm alerts so far. **No tool found** for auditing bundles or collecting authorizations.
- **Product:**
  1. **Audit (free → $49):** upload a Seller Central inventory/listings report (no Amazon API approval needed). The tool flags bundle ASINs and sorts each one: exempt / brand-packaged / needs authorization from these brands / convert to virtual bundle / sell through and remove. It shows the stock at risk and the sell-through deadline.
  2. **LOA outreach ($29–79/mo):** finds brand contacts, generates authorization letters, sends them for e-signature through Resend email, tracks replies and stores signed letters for Account Health appeals.
  3. **Done-for-you ($199+):** a human handles the outreach for large catalogs.
- **Distribution:** seller Facebook groups, YouTube, Amazon-seller podcasts, SEO on "Amazon bundle policy 2027" (heavy search right now), affiliate deals with seller-tool influencers.
- **After January:** expand into a general Amazon policy-change compliance tool. Next items: the Pan-EU FBA Belgium requirement (Feb 26, 2027) and the high-risk-category insurance rules (Nov 2, 2026).
- **Build:** 5–6 days for audit plus letter tracking. **Domains (unchecked):** bundleguard.com · bundleguard.app · bundleloa.com
- **Risks:**
  - A short, intense window of about 10 weeks.
  - Amazon could delay or soften the rule.
  - Brands may refuse to sign.
  - Big seller-tool suites (Helium 10, SellerAssistant) could add a checker quickly, so speed matters.

## CRA Ready (brief)
EU Cyber Resilience Act vulnerability reporting has been live since Sept 11, 2026; full obligations apply Dec 11, 2027 ([Crowell](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/its-live-the-cyber-resilience-act-reporting-is-mandatory-as-of-today-11-september-2026)). Small hardware and IoT makers and indie software vendors selling in the EU need software bills of materials (SBOMs), vulnerability handling and incident reporting. Software-composition tools are crowded, and doing this properly is hard.
