## 1. CourtMatch — gym time for clubs
- **Pain:** court space is the bottleneck. One basketball club is capped at about 200 kids by court time ([WWMT](https://wwmt.com/news/local/kalamazoo-indoor-sports-facility-photos-groundbreaking-youth-complex-tournaments-play-economic-impact-basketball-tennis-gymnastics-volleyball-courts-wwmt)). A Nebraska volleyball group says current space can't serve every child who wants to play ([KLIN, Oct 2026](https://klin.com/2026/10/15/proposed-lincoln-volleyball-complex-aims-to-expand-youth-access/)). Volleyball's boom means it competes with basketball for courts ([Sports Planning Guide](https://sportsplanningguide.com/state-of-volleyball/)).
- **Supply:** churches, private schools and community centers with gyms that sit empty on weeknights. Facilitron dominates *public* K-12 rentals, but churches and private schools mostly rent by phone.
- **Product:**
  - Gym owners list availability, rates and rules for free, with insurance-certificate upload and digital keys/check-in.
  - Clubs search a map, book recurring slots and pay through Stripe Connect.
  - 10–15% booking fee. Start in one metro: you hand-sign 20 gyms and sell to 30 clubs.
- **Build:** 7 days. **Domains (unchecked):** courtmatch.app · findcourttime.com · gymtime.app
- **Risks:** two-sided marketplace, a city at a time. Church boards move slowly. Liability insurance questions.

## 2. FairStay — tournament true cost and stay-to-play transparency
- **Pain:** parents are suing tournament housing firms. They say they were told their kids couldn't play unless they booked designated hotels, with fees on top ([Daily Caller, Oct 18 2026](https://dailycaller.com/2026/10/18/parents-sue-over-youth-sports-hotel-fees-and-booking-rules/), [Buying Sandlot](https://www.buyingsandlot.com/p/youth-sports-parents-sue-tournament-housing-firm-over-stay-to-play), [TulsaKids](https://www.tulsakids.com/how-stay-to-play-squeezes-sports-parents/)). Policies, buyout fees ($400+) and exemptions are buried in event fine print.
- **Product:** a searchable database of tournaments showing entry fee, stay-to-play policy, buyout and exemption rules, block rates versus open-market rates, and parent reviews. Clubs use it to choose events; parents get alerts. It's a programmatic SEO page for every tournament.
- **Money:** ads and sponsors, a $49/yr club "Pro" tier (season cost planner, team budgets), and later a club-run room-block service that undercuts housing firms.
- **Build:** 5 days, plus seeding data with AI extraction from event sites. **Domains (unchecked):** fairstay.org · staytoplay.info · tourneytruecost.com
- **Risks:** parents won't pay much (WTP 4/10). Tournament operators may push back legally. Revenue relies on audience scale.

## 3. CoachLaunch (brief)
Fewer than 20% of youth coaches have formal training ([Centro](https://withcentro.com/blog/volunteer-coach-onboarding-soccer)). Coach-facing AI apps are crowded (Pitch Planner, Hobbit AI, MyCantera, Practice Plan App). The less crowded angle is selling to **leagues**: one license per season covers onboarding, practice plans, playing-time fairness and SafeSport/concussion reminders for every volunteer coach.
