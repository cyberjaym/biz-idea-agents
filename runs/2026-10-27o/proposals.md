## 1. ClubSponsor
- **Gap:** no tool found that combines sponsor packages, local-business checkout, banner and jersey fulfilment, and renewals for youth clubs. Admin platforms (Jersey Watch, SportsEngine, TeamSnap, Spond) are general; sponsorship software (wehave, SponsorCX, Optimy) targets bigger properties ([wehave](https://www.wehave.io/insights/best-sponsorship-management-software-sports-clubs-2026), [Guideflow](https://www.guideflow.com/blog/sponsorship-management-software)).
- **Product:**
  - A public sponsor page with packages (e.g. field banner, jersey, "sponsor a player").
  - Stripe checkout, logo upload and invoice/receipt for the business.
  - A banner print-order tracker.
  - An end-of-season "thank you" report with banner photos.
  - Automatic renewal reminders.
  - AI-written pitch letters for local businesses.
- **Money:** 5% of sponsorship processed, or $199/season. Clubs pay out of money it helps them raise.
- **Build:** 5–6 days. **Why you:** your board does this by hand every season.

## 2. ClubFund
Adds a confidential aid desk: applications with document upload, reviewed only by the aid director, a scoring rubric and award tracking. Aid applications today are emailed PDFs or a PlayMetrics module ([AFC](https://www.associationfc.org/financial-aid), [Telluride](https://www.telluridesoccer.com/financial-aid/), [NCFC](https://www.ncfcyouth.com/financial-aid)). It links "sponsor a player" money directly to scholarships. Cost is the top barrier: 66% of Latino families struggle to afford youth sports ([Aspen](https://www.aspeninstitute.org/blog-posts/latinos-soccer-whats-us-model-theyre-not-left-behind/)).

## 3. Bilingüe
Language barriers and limited bilingual outreach are documented ([Aspen](https://www.aspeninstitute.org/blog-posts/latinos-soccer-whats-us-model-theyre-not-left-behind/), [ResearchGate study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/396682996_Challenges_and_Opportunities_for_Latine_Families_in_US_Youth_Sport), [LeagueApps](https://leagueapps.com/blog/youth-sports-in-hispanic-communities/)). Product: CoachLaunch-style plans in Spanish for parent coaches, plus soccer-aware translation of club emails, texts and forms. Low willingness to pay; could be funded by state associations and foundation equity grants.
